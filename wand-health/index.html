Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Wand directly.

What does it cost?
$200/monthDPC membership

No copays, no insurance billing and no surprise fees. Membership is month-to-month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Whether you can pay with an HSA or FSA depends on tax rules and your plan, so confirm with your plan administrator. Dr. Wand can provide receipts on request.

What's included?
  • Office visits, telehealth, and home visits when appropriate
  • Acute, chronic and preventive care, with same- or next-day availability when possible
  • Direct text access between visits through secure messaging
  • PrEP and HIV care
  • Weight, metabolic and nutrition support
  • Care coordination and discounted labs and medications
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Wand Health doesn't bill insurance for membership or visits. Dr. Wand recommends keeping a plan for hospital stays, surgery, specialist care and emergencies.

Wand Health isn't currently enrolled in Medicare, so Medicare patients can't be seen at this time. Dr. Wand plans to offer it later in 2026 and keeps a waitlist.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

Labs, medications, imaging and specialist services are generally separate from the membership. Insurance can often be used for them, or Dr. Wand has discounted pricing on many labs, imaging studies and medications, which can be cheaper than using insurance.

For PrEP, he helps patients with insurance coverage and manufacturer assistance programs so that costs can often come close to $0.

How does the introductory call work?

You pick a time for a free 15-minute call to ask questions and see if Wand Health is a good fit. If you decide to join, you sign up for the monthly membership. Times are in Pacific time, and if none works you can email care@wandmd.com.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Membership is month-to-month with no long-term contract, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Who can join?

Wand Health sees California patients only and is cash-pay. It is an LGBTQ+ affirming practice, and care includes PrEP, HIV prevention and treatment, and gender-affirming support.
