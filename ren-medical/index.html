Do you take insurance?

Ren does not bill insurance for membership services. The membership works alongside your insurance, and you can use it for anything that is ordered (labs, imaging), prescribed (medications) or referred (specialists). Depending on your plan and insurance status, insurance may be billed for covered medical services.

Can I join with Medicare, Medicaid or Tricare?

Not at this time. Government plans are all or none, so people with Medicare, Medicaid, Tricare or similar coverage are not eligible for membership or other medical services at Ren. The one exception is aesthetics. If you are enrolled in a federal healthcare program, contact the office before joining.

Is Ren direct primary care?

Not exactly. Ren is a hybrid, access-based model that combines primary care, preventive and executive health, longevity and optimization, and aesthetic medicine.

Is there a consult fee?

For the weight optimization and hormone optimization programs, the initial consult is $200 and credits toward your first month of membership. Labs are separate.
