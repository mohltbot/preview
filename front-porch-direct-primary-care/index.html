Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Kessler directly.

What does it cost?
$125/monthIndividual, all ages
$225/monthPair, two members of the same family or long-term partners
$300/monthFamily, up to 2 parents and their children ages 0 to 25

The monthly fee covers all your care with Dr. Kessler. Since 1/1/2026 you can use HSA contributions to pay for a direct primary care membership.

If the cost is a barrier, ask Dr. Kessler whether a reduced-cost membership is available.

What's included?
  • Unlimited visits with your doctor, 30 to 60 minutes each
  • Same-day or next-day appointments for urgent issues
  • Routine preventive care, wellness visits and chronic disease management
  • Telehealth and phone visits, plus calls and texts directly to your doctor
  • Self-scheduling for follow-up and urgent visits
  • In-office procedures, and basic testing such as urine, diabetic testing, rapid flu, COVID, RSV and strep, and EKG
  • Medication management and prescription refills
  • Deeply discounted lab testing and imaging, and personalized care coordination
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Front Porch doesn't bill insurance. Membership isn't insurance either, and Dr. Kessler recommends having a plan for a severe emergency, such as traditional insurance or a health share. She's happy to see you if you don't have insurance.

If you have an HMO plan, ask your insurer whether an out-of-network provider can order labs, imaging and referrals.

How do I pay for labs and imaging?

You have 2 options. Pay the discounted cash rate, which is often a fraction of the cost even with insurance. Or have the order sent on a traditional requisition and billed through your insurance.

How do I get started?

Schedule a meet and greet on the online calendar to talk with Dr. Kessler about the practice. If you're not sure which plan fits your household, ask her directly.

Who can join on each plan?

All ages are eligible. A Pair membership can be a parent and child, a couple, or 2 children from the same family. A Family membership covers up to 2 adults and their children ages 0 to 25. If you have a blended family, ask Dr. Kessler whether it fits.
