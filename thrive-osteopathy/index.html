Questions before you join

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Zerba directly.

Office visits are included, so you never pay extra to see the doctor. There is a one-time $100 registration fee to get your records and set you up in the system. If you sign up for the year, you get 1 month free.

A few things are priced separately, such as osteopathic manual therapy at $100 a visit for members. Full prices are on the Thrive Osteopathy pricing page.

What's included?
  • Unrestricted office visits, typically with a next-day appointment
  • Visits that usually run 30 minutes and can go as long as 90
  • Annual physical exam
  • Phone, text and email with Dr. Zerba
  • Coordination with specialists
  • Laceration repair, cryotherapy for skin lesions and warts, and skin biopsy, with supplies added at wholesale prices
  • Care for kids and adults, including chronic conditions, mental health, sports medicine and urgent care
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Thrive Osteopathy doesn't accept or bill insurance, and that includes Medicare. People with Medicare can still join, and you can still use Medicare for imaging, labs and referrals. You can join with no insurance at all.

The practice recommends keeping a major medical plan, ideally a high-deductible plan with a health savings account, for a serious health problem. Its FAQ says that since 1/1/2026 HSA money can pay DPC membership fees if you have a qualifying high-deductible plan, up to monthly caps.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

Labs and medications are at wholesale prices. The practice dispenses select medications in the office, and says its price for nearly all of them is below a pharmacy co-pay. It lists annual bloodwork for under $50 and an MRI for under $1,000.

Special labs and procedures may carry an extra fee, and you'll know what it is before you go ahead.

How does joining work?

The practice has an active waitlist and enrolls new patients each month. You fill out the sign-up form to be added, and the practice contacts you when the next enrollment spot opens.

Once you're a member, you can schedule visits yourself through the Atlas patient hub online or the Atlas app.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The FAQ says you're free to cancel your membership at any time. If you decide to rejoin later, the FAQ lists a $200 registration fee.

Who can join?

Adults and children. Children need to be on a plan with at least one adult member. The practice keeps its patient list small (under 500) so there's more time for each person.
