Questions before you join

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Ali directly.

Children under 18 are $45 a month and need a parent enrolled. There is a $50 registration fee and a one-month deposit due with your first month's payment. Family packets are available, so contact the practice for details.

What's included?
  • Annual physical and wellbeing exam
  • Chronic disease management for conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, asthma and COPD
  • On-site testing for flu, COVID-19, strep, glucose and ECGs, at no extra cost
  • Minor procedures such as stitches, abscess drainage and earwax removal
  • Same-day or next-day sick visits, plus phone, text or email access to your doctor, even after hours
  • Specialist referrals coordinated and scheduled for you
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

The practice runs on a monthly membership, with no insurance or copays required and no surprise bills.

Specialists outside the practice, hospital and emergency room visits, urgent care and surgery are not part of the membership.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

In-house tests are included. External laboratory tests, imaging and diagnostic procedures, and pharmacy medications are not part of the membership.

The practice gives members steep discounts on commonly prescribed medications and labs through trusted partners.

How does joining work?

Use the enroll page to sign up. You can also call or send a message through the practice's contact form with questions first. Children under 18 are enrolled by a parent.

Who is it for?

Families, individuals with a high deductible, seniors, people managing a chronic condition, and small business owners or employees. The practice also offers immigration physicals, which are not part of the membership.
