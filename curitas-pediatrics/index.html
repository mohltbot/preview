Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Alex directly.

What does it cost?
$450/monthMembership, ages 0 to 5
$370/monthMembership, ages 6 to 11
$325/monthMembership, ages 12 to 18

There is a one-time, non-refundable registration fee of $500 per family. Paying annually gets a 5% discount, and families with 3 or more children get 20% off the youngest child.

Without a membership, a virtual sick consult is $150 and a house call is $500 ($250 for each additional sibling). School and sports physicals are also $500 per house call.

What's included?
  • Direct access to Dr. Alex by call, text or email, even after hours
  • In-home wellness visits and sick visits with same- or next-day availability
  • House calls each year: 8 for ages 0 to 5, 5 for ages 6 to 11 and 3 for ages 12 to 18. Additional visits are $350, and some nearby towns may qualify for unlimited visits
  • Point-of-care testing, common minor procedures and select medications
  • Childhood vaccines through the practice's partnership with My Concierge Pharmacy
  • School, camp and sports forms at no extra cost
  • Monthly pharmacist consultations and home delivery of prescriptions
  • Chronic condition management and care coordination
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Curitas doesn't accept insurance. It does accept HSA and FSA cards, depending on your plan's guidelines, plus credit card or bank account payments.

The practice recommends keeping health insurance for labs, imaging, ER visits, hospital stays, vaccines and medications.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

Point-of-care testing for diagnostics or screening is included in membership. Labs and imaging go through your insurance.

Dr. Alex can send a prescription to your pharmacy, and some medications may be available during the visit. Members can also get prescriptions delivered to their home through My Concierge Pharmacy.

How do I join?

You schedule a free virtual meet and greet. It's a relaxed way to ask questions and decide if the practice is the right fit. If you'd like to try the practice first, there are one-time sick visits and physicals, and a Newborn Care Package for babies under 60 days old.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time with 30 days' notice. Prepaid memberships are refundable on a prorated basis within 30 days of cancellation.

Who does Dr. Alex see, and where?

Children from birth through age 18. Care is at home across northern Palm Beach County and southern Martin County. If you aren't sure you're in the area, contact the practice and they'll clarify. Dr. Alex is fluent in English and Spanish.
