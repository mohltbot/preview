What's included?
  • Same-day and next-day visits for illness or injury

Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Direct Primary Care takes insurance billing out of the exam room, and the practice says no insurance is required to join. Your membership covers your relationship with your physician directly. All are welcome.

What about labs and other services?

Members receive labs and additional services at a reduced, transparent rate.

How does joining work?

You can enroll online with the practice's enrollment link. If you'd like to be contacted first, fill out the Get Started form with your name, email and phone, and the practice will be in touch to get you started.

You can also email Dr. Oldenkamp or call (860) 955-5676. The practice asks that you leave personal health information out of email.
