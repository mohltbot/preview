Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Ali directly.

What does it cost?
$185/monthPer child with Dr. Kristen. 4 month minimum, and families pay no more than $450 a month.
$300/monthPer child for new patients of Dr. Ali. 12 month commitment.

Families pay a monthly fee for unlimited access to all services. Paying annually saves 10%, which is more than 1 month free.

Dr. Ali is currently accepting newborns, children and teens with complex or precision medicine needs, and siblings of current patients. Dr. Kristen is accepting new families, newborns through college students.

What's included?
  • Direct access to your pediatrician by text, email or call, and flexible scheduling
  • Wellness exams on the AAP schedule, and school and sports physicals
  • Acute care for illness and injury, in office, in home or by telehealth, with routine tests such as strep and flu
  • Minor procedures such as laceration repair, wound care and splinting
  • Mental and emotional health support, including management of ADHD, depression, anxiety and behavior concerns
  • Basic medications and labs, included or at cost
  • Ear piercing at no additional charge for members
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

No. Membership is self-pay. The practice encourages members to keep health insurance for emergency care, specialists, hospitalization and imaging.

Vaccines are the exception. They are billed through insurance by the partner company Vaxcare, and most private insurances are in network, so enter your insurance during enrollment.

What about vaccines, labs and medications?

The practice provides all childhood vaccines, including COVID and flu. It encourages vaccination on the AAP schedule but doesn't require it.

Basic labs are included or at cost, and you can walk out of the office with medications in hand, also included or at cost.

How do I join?

You book a free 20-minute meet and greet, virtual, by phone or in person. A meet and greet is required before enrolling with Dr. Ali. Enrollment then happens in the SigmaMD app, where you start with your own information and add each child as a member. Your first month's payment is charged on enrollment.

Who can join?

Newborns through college students. There are membership options for infants and for teens and college students, and Dr. Ali also offers a precision medicine and complex care membership. Visits are in the office, at home or by telehealth.
