Questions before you join

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Matthews directly.

What does it cost?
$100/monthYoung adults 14 to 25
$150/monthAdults 26 to 64
$200/monthAdults 65 and up
$125/monthRemote access, ages 26 and up

Billing is automatic, monthly or yearly. There is no bundled family rate, and family members you refer earn a one-time account credit equal to their monthly price.

What's included?
  • Unlimited office visits of 30 to 60 minutes, in person or virtual
  • Direct text and email communication with your doctor anytime
  • Special request office hours and visits
  • Select in-office procedures
  • In-office diagnostic testing, including point-of-care ultrasound

Wholesale lab pricing, skin procedures, pathology and imaging at select centers are available at contracted rates.

Can I use insurance?

Highpoint doesn't bill insurance for anything done in the office, and insurance can't be used for the membership fee. You can use your insurance for outside services like labs, imaging, vaccines and prescriptions. The doctors recommend keeping insurance for emergencies and high-cost procedures. HSA and FSA eligibility depends on your plan, so check with your plan administrator.

How are labs handled?

Labs go through Quest Diagnostics at the practice's wholesale pricing, with prices shown upfront. You can still use your insurance for labs if you prefer.

How does enrollment work?
  • Join the waitlist by email. An enrollment link comes by email once space is available.
  • Complete the enrollment form and add a credit card. It isn't charged until your enrollment is confirmed.
  • One of the doctors confirms your information and activates your membership within 1 to 2 business days.
  • Then you can schedule your first visit.
Is there a contract?

Memberships are 12-month agreements with no cancellation fee. Rejoining after you leave has a one-time $450 re-enrollment fee.

Who can join?

Children 14 and older and adults of all ages who live in Colorado. Remote access members can join from Washington (with either doctor) and from Arizona, Utah, Montana and Wyoming (with Dr. Bross). Dr. Bross isn't taking new members right now, so the waitlist is for Dr. Matthews.
