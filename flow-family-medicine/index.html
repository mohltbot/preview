Questions before you join

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Goldburt directly.

No copays for office visits. There is a one-time, non-refundable $75 enrollment fee per person. The 2nd and 3rd family members get 5% off, a family of 4 pays $350 a month at most, and each additional member is $50 a month.

Billing is monthly by credit card or direct deposit, and the first payment is billed at your first scheduled visit. HSA funds can pay membership fees up to $150 per person or $350 per family a month, and many FSAs reimburse them too (confirm with your employer). Households under 300% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines can ask about reduced rates.

What's included?
  • Extended visits for preventive care, chronic disease management and long-term planning
  • Same-day or next-day access for acute illness
  • Direct contact with Dr. Goldburt by phone, text or secure messaging
  • Check-ups, well woman care, well child care and peri-menopausal care
  • Point-of-care tests, including pregnancy, urine dip, strep, and rapid flu and COVID
  • In-office procedures such as wart and skin tag removal, skin biopsies, simple laceration repair, joint injections, and IUD and implant placement and removal
  • Specialist e-consults, at no extra cost
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Flow has opted out of all insurance networks and doesn't bill insurance for membership. You still need coverage for emergencies, hospital stays and specialist care, and the practice accepts you as a patient whatever insurance you have.

PPO plans, traditional Medicare, Medicare Advantage PPOs and MassHealth Standard work well because they don't make you pick an in-network primary care doctor. Private HMOs, Medicare Advantage HMOs and most MassHealth ACOs do require one, so book a Meet & Greet to talk those through.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

Medications, outside labs and imaging aren't part of the membership. Most people use their insurance for them, and the practice helps arrange them with providers it has negotiated cash prices with, often lower than insurance rates.

When you need a specialist, Dr. Goldburt can send a case summary to a panel of specialists. In about 80% of cases that answers the question without a separate specialist visit, and it's included in your membership.

How does the Meet & Greet work?

It's a short, free introductory call with Dr. Goldburt to see if Flow is the right fit. You pick a time online.

If it feels right, you schedule an onboarding meeting to sign up for membership and go over your health history and goals. After that you can message, book visits and get care without copays.

Who can join?

Individuals and families, from newborns to older adults. Dr. Goldburt describes cradle-to-grave family care, including newborn care, and the office is designed to be quiet and sensory friendly.

Primary care is only offered through membership. One-time visits aren't available for primary care. The practice supports vaccination, and says that if you don't plan to vaccinate your child at all, it's probably not the best fit.
