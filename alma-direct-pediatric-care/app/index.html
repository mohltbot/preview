A Alma app for your members

Your members message you, book visits and manage their membership in one app with your name and colors on it. Billing and records can stay in SigmaMD.

Sample screens. Names, times and messages are made up; prices and visit types come from almadpc.com.

What we'd build

Included

  • An iPhone and Android app with your name, logo and colors
  • Messaging between members and you, with notifications
  • Visit booking that members do themselves
  • Membership, payments and family members, kept in sync with SigmaMD
  • Setup, the app store listings and 30 days of changes after launch

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't replace SigmaMD. Billing and records stay there.
  • Nothing changes for your members until you approve the app.
