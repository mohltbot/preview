Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Jaleh directly.

What does it cost?
$25/monthChildren, ages 0 to 18
$85/monthAdults, ages 19 to 65
$100/monthSeniors, over 65

Memberships can be billed monthly, quarterly or annually. Some plans allow FSA and HSA money to pay for membership.

Limited financial assistance is available through the practice's community fund and a discretionary sliding scale.

What's included?
  • Visits in the office, by video or by phone, with 30 to 60 minutes with your doctor
  • Same-day or next-day visits for urgent concerns
  • Direct phone, text and email access to your doctor
  • In-office procedures such as skin biopsies, wart removal, stitches, IUD and Nexplanon placement and removal, and nebulizer treatments
  • Wholesale pricing on medications and reduced cost on labs
Do I need insurance?

Lote Tree does not bill insurance. Dr. Rahimi recommends keeping health insurance for major medical events such as surgeries and emergencies, which membership does not cover.

If you have an Oregon-regulated plan (not an HMO such as Kaiser), you can request that your monthly payments count toward your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum under Oregon HB 2540.

What about labs and medications?

Labs, imaging and prescription medications are not included in the membership fee. Members get wholesale pricing on some medications and reduced cost on labs, and you know the price before you receive a service.

How does the meet and greet work?

You book a free 10 minute meet and greet online and talk with Dr. Rahimi about the practice. After that you schedule an initial visit and complete your intake forms.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There are no pro-rated refunds. If you re-enroll within 90 days, your membership is reactivated at no additional cost. After 90 days, a re-enrollment fee applies.

Who can join?

Anyone. Dr. Rahimi sees children, adults and seniors, and speaks English and Spanish.
