Questions before you join

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Imanirad directly.

What does it cost?
$100/monthSilver, or $1,100 a year
$150/monthGold, or $1,750 a year
$250/monthPlatinum, or $2,800 a year
$280/visitInitial allergy consultation, 90 minutes, no membership needed

Silver covers up to 3 visits a year, Gold up to 5, and Platinum is built around monthly visit options or Q&A sessions, with visit frequency set by how complex your care is. Silver members get 10% off allergy immunotherapy, Gold members 20%.

Follow-up visits without a membership are $180 for up to 40 minutes. Testing, immunotherapy and complex-care visits are priced separately as fee for service.

What's included?
  • Silver: up to 3 visits a year based on clinical need, for stable allergic conditions, with discounted labs, medications and supplements
  • Gold: up to 5 visits a year, direct access to Dr. Imanirad's phone, priority scheduling and proactive follow-up, spirometry as clinically indicated, and personalized, integrative treatment planning and care coordination
  • Platinum: monthly visit options or 1:1 Q&A sessions with Dr. Imanirad or the support team, for complex, multi-system allergic and inflammatory conditions, with advanced gut and systems biology evaluation and discounts on advanced functional labs
Do you take insurance?

No. Elaria is a direct specialty care practice and doesn't accept insurance. All consultations and in-office services are direct-pay, and you pay at the time of service.

Many laboratory tests, imaging studies and prescriptions ordered outside your visit may still go through your insurance, depending on your coverage.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

All three membership levels include discounted labs, medications and supplements. Platinum adds discounts on advanced functional labs.

Labs, imaging and prescriptions ordered outside your visit may still be processed through your insurance.

How does joining work?

You fill out the membership request form on the Elaria membership page and choose Silver, Gold or Platinum. If you'd rather start with one visit, you can pick a visit type in the online scheduler. Payment is due when you book.

Members and known patients who need a time outside the scheduler can call 813-773-3499.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact the practice at least 2 business days before your appointment. No-shows and cancellations less than 24 hours ahead are subject to a $100 fee, and the rest of your payment is refunded.

Who is it for?

Children, teens and adults. Elaria sees people with known allergies, including environmental allergies, food allergies, eczema, asthma and eosinophilic esophagitis, and people who haven't gotten a clear diagnosis for their symptoms.
