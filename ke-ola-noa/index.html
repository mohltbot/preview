Questions before you book

The answers people ask for most. Anything else, ask Dr. Donna directly.

What does it cost?
$200/monthMonthly membership, ages 14 to 99

No copays and no minimum contract length. The only other fee is a $150 re-enrollment fee if you leave and come back. There are no late fees or no-show fees.

Membership may be payable with HSA or FSA funds, depending on your plan. Ask your insurer or employer. Dr. Donna's team can send an invoice. Discounts are available for members who refer others, teachers, first responders and military, and the practice offers limited financial assistance or sliding-scale options depending on availability.

  • Direct contact with Dr. Donna by text, phone or email
  • Annual physicals and lifestyle and wellness planning
  • Chronic disease management
  • Basic in-office procedures, such as EKGs and minor skin procedures
  • Common medications for conditions such as high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol, thyroid conditions, allergies, hormone imbalances and bacterial infections (since April 2026)
  • Weekly yoga, monthly cooking classes and monthly nature walks
Do you take insurance, and do I still need it?

Ke Ola Noa doesn't bill any insurance. All of its services are covered by the monthly membership. It cares for patients with all types of insurance (HMSA, Kaiser, Medicare, Med-QUEST and others) and for people with none.

You still need coverage for anything outside primary care, like hospital stays, surgery and specialist visits. Medicare and Med-QUEST patients can join at the standard rate by signing a waiver that they won't bill their insurer for services received at Ke Ola Noa.

What about labs, imaging and prescriptions?

The practice helps members find lower cash prices for labs, imaging and many common medications, often well below what they'd pay going through insurance. You can still use your insurance for any of them.

Many common generic medications come with free home delivery through a pharmacy partner. If you already take medications, Dr. Donna will review your list and help move eligible prescriptions into the program.

How does the discovery call work?

You pick a time online for a free 15-minute virtual visit with Dr. Donna. If you want to join, the practice sends you a link to sign up for membership. After that you get access to your secure patient portal to schedule your first visit, where she gets to know you and your goals and builds a personalized plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Membership is month-to-month with no long-term contract and no cancellation fee. If you return later, a one-time $150 re-enrollment fee likely applies.
