No copays and no surprise bills. A UMM college student rate (ages 18 to 25) is $360 per semester. If you pay 12 months in full with your first month, you save $5 a month per member.

The Hormone Health Program is $499 for 6 months of unlimited hormone health care, including the initial consult, follow-up visits, telehealth, and phone, text and email support.

What's included?
  • Unlimited office visits, with same-day or next-day appointments for urgent concerns
  • Visits of 30 to 60 minutes
  • Calling, texting or emailing your doctor directly
  • Annual wellness exams, preventive care and chronic disease management
  • In-office procedures, including skin tag and mole removal, joint injections and laceration repair
  • Coordination of specialty care, with referrals and follow-ups
  • Discounted labs and wholesale medications
Do you take insurance?

Willow Creek Health works outside the traditional insurance system. A flat monthly fee covers unlimited visits, with no co-pays. Ask the practice at your meet and greet how your own coverage fits with membership.

What about labs and prescriptions?

Membership includes discounted labs with clear, upfront prices for necessary tests, and wholesale prices on medications.

How does joining work?

You schedule a meet and greet with Dr. Meichsner to talk through your health care needs and see if membership is a fit. When you're ready, you become a member through the online enrollment link.

Who can join?

The whole family. Willow Creek offers care for all ages, from well-child visits and school, camp and sports physicals to well-woman visits and care for adults 65 and over. It also offers a Fourth Trimester Care Plan with home visits for new moms and babies.
